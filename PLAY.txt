PUNCH TAP! - PLAYABLE RECOVERED FIRST FIGHT
=========================================
Open project.godot in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5, or run:
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\tools\play.ps1

Mobile: left joystick, LEFT and RIGHT punches, SPECIAL.
Joystick up + punch = head; neutral + punch = body; down = guard;
sides = slip. Release and tap again for another action.
Keyboard: J/K punch, W aim high, S guard, A/D slip, Space special.
Enter starts/resumes/rematches; Escape pauses; SFX toggles sound.

Idle head shots land; body shots need an opening. Watch the taunt and guard.
Slips take six frames to become protective, so react before contact.
Hearts are not regenerating stamina: blocked shots cost one, hits taken cost
three. If exhausted, evade to recover hearts. Stars power the delayed uppercut;
one star is sufficient. Stars come from qualifying hits, not three counters.
When knocked down, repeatedly tap the punch buttons once the get-up cue appears.
Three knockdowns in one round cause TKO. Ordinary knockdowns can recover.
After three rounds, 5,000 points wins the decision.

The game uses measured first-opponent mechanics and original Nico Reyes art.
Full animation bytecode and NES RNG are not yet ported; whole-bout frame parity
is not claimed. See research/RECOVERY_UPDATE.txt for evidence and limitations.
Windows playable verified; Android/iOS export and real-device testing remain.
