Measured positions are VISIBLE pixel bounds, not collision boxes. Player
measurement excludes HUD/ring outside x40..215/y90..239 and selects the
connected fighter component near the captured center lane. This excludes the
background-layer referee during knockdown counts. $61 in those intermission
states can select referee/background updates, not just Mac poses. Opponent measurement
uses sprites within +/-52 pixels of its anchor to exclude the distant referee.
Visible off-screen clipping is retained. The uppermost glove-colour pixel row
is recorded as a reach marker. It is not an independently decoded hitbox.
Player layer uses palette RGB(92,228,48); opponent glove RGB(181,49,32).
Normal background is RGB(21,95,217); a clear ring sample tracks its colour
during fades, avoiding false full-screen actor bounds. Images are read, never
edited for measurement. Offscreen extent is restored only when the visible
mask exactly matches a prefix/suffix of a captured full silhouette. Art is
then cropped at that extent instead of shrinking the entire body. Ambiguous
extents are flagged in evidence/animation/clipping_validation.json.
1595 actor samples had recoverable clipped extent; 55 player fall samples
remain unresolved. They are visible measurements only, not full-body bounds.

GODOT CHANGES
scripts/recovered/animation_timeline.gd selects source samples by simulation
age. main.gd no longer adds sine bob, anticipation shake, authored taunt
scaling, linear dodge movement, or the contact-minus-five pose window.
It maps measured NES coordinates into the portrait arena with one uniform
2.8x coordinate transform and fits original-art regions into measured bounds.
Get-up tails are selected after the live count/input-completion event; timing
is not stretched to fit. Idle currently reuses a captured variant, not NES RNG.
Existing controls and gameplay rules remain. Original art remains separate
from ROM images; the production game requires neither ROM nor emulator.

VALIDATION
animation_tracks_test.gd independently rereads raw RAM and checks 3268 paired
samples against player pose/scroll/contact, opponent sequence key and anchor:
zero mismatches. UI/input tests: 21 pass; scripted bout still ends TKO with
48 player HP, 8550 points and 10089 simulated frames.
These checks do NOT certify all animation branches, all limb placements or
independent whole-bout frame parity. Do not cite them as such.

REMAINING WORK — NOT HIDDEN BEHIND A PASS LABEL
1. Original artwork has eight poses per actor. The measured inventory has
   many more selectors and rendered variants, including intermission states.
   Current _art_pose correspondence is coarse and marked as such per frame.
   Several source poses share art; walk, individual recoil/guard transitions,
   punch anticipation/recovery and get-up silhouettes need distinct artwork.
2. Fitting visible bounds does not recover an anatomical skeleton. No warped
   body mesh is used to manufacture a glove match. Actual art glove-marker
   error is displayed and saved in comparison/registration_errors.json.
3. Full conditional animation bytecode execution, NES RNG and interruption
   branch coverage remain. Recorded samples prove those observed paths only.
   Live contact/reaction frame alignment must also be checked independently;
   the clip-replay comparison cannot certify the game's event-dispatch phase.
4. Artwork registration, complete sequence execution and live game validation
   must pass before calling this a finished faithful animation port.
